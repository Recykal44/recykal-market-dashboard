Data quality notes — what's been checked and fixed

Refreshed 20 Sep 2026 via the single-command full_daily_refresh.py pipeline: 322 onboarded (43 marketing-attributed, 114 legacy-MIP matches), 347 qualifying shipments (₹5.04 Cr), 945 listings, 1,280 Workbench leads.

Lead Journey rebuilt as a snapshot (20 Sep): both "POC Since" and "Stage Since" turned out to be periodically bulk-overwritten for many leads at once, at every cluster size (only ~5% of timestamps are genuinely one lead per minute) — not just the big spikes a first fix (19 Sep) tried to filter out. Rather than keep patching a fabricated day-by-day timeline, the panel now shows a live snapshot of Call Status / Lead Status / Stage instead — plain current field values, not reconstructed events.

Two Workbench fields remain unreliable and are used only where explicitly requested, with this caveat: "Lead Created" can show impossible future dates; "Added to Workbench" is a frozen batch-refresh stamp, not live. "Stage Since" is not used for any date-based figure for the same reason.

Revenue methodology: Source A "Overall Shipments" tab, Invoice_Total_Amount, deduped by Shipment_ID, Order_Status in ISSUED/IN_PROGRESS/DELIVERED/COMPLETED. Window revenue reflects shipment activity of companies onboarded in that window (a cohort view), not shipments dated in that window.

Sources: Open Marketplace Business Dashboard (Buyer/Seller Onboarding, Overall Shipments, Listings), ct dashboard (Raw_OB_Buyers/Raw_OB_CT), Marketing Leads Workbench, legacy MIP database. All figures reflect a live pull via full xlsx/csv export.
